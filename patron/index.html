Become a patron

I've been writing songs since 1985, more than 900 of them, many about the towns, rivers and people of Western Massachusetts. The songs that haven't been written yet need time, and time is what a patron gives.

Hear one of them: The Hadley Witch.

And the newest: Hollow in the Pines.

There are no tiers and no rewards. You give what feels right, once or every month, and the work goes on.

What you do get is me. Meet me for coffee. Call and ask how a song came together. Tell me about a story you think deserves a song, or a style you'd like to hear me take on. I'll listen.

If you'd like to support the songs I've already recorded, you can do that as a patron on Bandcamp. This page is for what comes next.
