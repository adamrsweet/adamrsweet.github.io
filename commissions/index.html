A song of your own

Commission a song.

Want a song for your town, event, business, family or cause? There are three ways to commission one.

A song that's yours, from $3,800. You own it outright and use it however you like, and it stays out of my catalog.

A shared song, from $2,500. Your name goes in the credits, and you can use the song free at events, on your website, in videos and on social media. You can't sell it, license it or claim it as yours. I keep the song, and it joins my catalog for everyone to hear.

Songwriting only, from $1,200. You get the lyrics, melody and chords, written out so your own musicians can play it.

Terms

Prices for recorded songs include musicians and studio. Your quote lists my fee and the musician and studio costs for your song separately. Musician and studio costs are quoted at signing and valid for 30 days.

Half the total quote is paid up front, and the rest before the final files are delivered.

Recorded songs include one round of revisions on the lyrics and one on the final mix. Songwriting only includes one round on the lyrics. More rounds are quoted separately.

If you decide not to go ahead after work begins, I keep the deposit and you owe nothing more. I keep the song, and a shared-song client no longer has the right to use it.

With songwriting only, I keep the song and you can record and perform it whenever you like. Any recording credits it as written by Adam Sweet. To own it outright, it's $1,800.

Final terms are in the agreement you sign.
