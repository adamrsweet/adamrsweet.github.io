Songs and their stories

The Hadley Witch

In the winter of 1684-85, Mary Webster of Hadley was hanged from a tree by her own neighbors and buried in the snow. In the morning she was still alive. This song tells her story, a few miles from where it happened.

The Road to Hamelin

Raymond Beaudoin was born in Holyoke in 1918, a mill-town kid who grew up along the canals. On April 6, 1945, his platoon was pinned down in an open field near Hamelin, Germany, the town of the Pied Piper legend, and he went forward alone so his men could make it through. He was twenty-six. He didn't come home, and he was awarded the Medal of Honor for that day.

The Lost Battalion

Charles Whittlesey grew up in Pittsfield, a quiet young man in wire-rimmed glasses who'd rather read than fight. In October 1918 he led 554 men into a ravine in the Argonne Forest, where they were cut off for five days with no food or water, and 194 of them walked out. This one is for every quiet veteran who doesn't talk about it.

Hollow in the Pines

This song carries the weight of what we bury and what still finds its way back, through dreams and memories. It's about a place that holds your secrets, even when you'd rather they be forgotten. For anyone who's ever felt tethered to somewhere dark but familiar, this one's for you.
