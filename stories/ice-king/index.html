New Song: Ice King

Some of the best song ideas come from historical stories that sound too unbelievable to be true.

When I first read about Frederic Tudor, I thought it had to be an exaggeration. Here was a man in the early 1800s who believed he could harvest ice from frozen Massachusetts ponds and ship it halfway around the world to some of the hottest places on Earth.

Most people thought he was crazy.

What made the story even more remarkable was that he succeeded. Using sawdust as insulation, Tudor transported ice across oceans and through tropical climates. In 1833, one of his shipments arrived in Calcutta, India, proving that natural ice could survive a journey of thousands of miles.

The story has all the ingredients of a great song. A dreamer with an impossible idea. Skeptics who said it couldn’t be done. Long ocean voyages. Ingenuity and persistence. And ultimately, success.

“Ice King” tells that story. The song celebrates the spirit of innovation and the willingness to pursue an idea even when everyone around you thinks it will fail.

Frederic Tudor earned the nickname “The Ice King,” and more than a century later his story remains a reminder that some of the world’s biggest changes begin with an idea that sounds completely unreasonable.
