New Song: Seneca Drums

For more than three hundred years, people living around Seneca Lake in upstate New York have heard strange, powerful booms coming from the water. These sounds were so loud they could be heard for miles, sometimes shaking windows and waking people in the night.

The Seneca people believed they were hearing the voice of the Great Spirit. Later settlers told stories of ghostly warriors. Even the writer James Fenimore Cooper wrote about the strange “lake gun” sounds. Many people wondered if the booms came from aliens or secret military tests.

Scientists have now solved the mystery. The booms are caused by large bubbles of methane gas that build up under the lakebed. When they finally break free and reach the surface, they explode with a loud cannon-like sound. The deep waters of the lake make the noise even louder.

I wrote “Seneca Drums” to tell this story. It honors the long history of wonder around the lake and the natural power hidden beneath its calm surface.
