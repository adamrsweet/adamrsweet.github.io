New Song: When El Niño Comes To Town

For this song, I was drawn to a story that spans oceans and continents.

El Niño began as an observation made by Peruvian fishermen who noticed warm waters arriving near Christmas and changing the fish populations they depended upon. Over time, scientists discovered that this was part of a much larger system linking the ocean and atmosphere across vast distances. What happens in one part of the Pacific can influence rainfall, drought, temperature, and weather patterns around the globe.

As a songwriter, I found that idea irresistible. There is something musical about the way these connections work. One change in the ocean leads to another in the atmosphere. A shift in the winds affects rainfall thousands of miles away. The entire system behaves almost like an orchestra, with different sections responding to one another across great distances.

“When El Niño Comes to Town” grew from that image. Rather than focusing on disaster or uncertainty, the song celebrates the wonder of a living planet whose oceans, winds, and clouds remain deeply connected.

Sometimes the most remarkable stories are not about people at all. Sometimes they are about the invisible forces that connect us all through the air above us and the waters that surround us.
