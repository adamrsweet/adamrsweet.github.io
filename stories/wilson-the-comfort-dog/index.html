New Song: Wilson, The Comfort Dog

Every once in a while a news story comes along that practically writes its own song.

When I read about Wilson, the comfort dog recently sworn in as a member of the Amherst Fire Department, I couldn’t stop smiling. At just nine weeks old, Wilson took an oath to provide comfort, companionship, and unconditional love to first responders and the community they serve.

The story is heartwarming, but it also highlights something important. Firefighters, police officers, paramedics, and other first responders often face difficult situations that can take an emotional toll. Comfort dogs provide support in a simple but powerful way. Sometimes a calm presence, a friendly face, and a wagging tail can make a real difference.

What struck me most was how many people came together to make Wilson’s arrival possible. From his breeders and trainers to department leaders, donors, and community members, the project reflects the kind of teamwork that helps small towns thrive.

“Wilson, the Comfort Dog” is my attempt to capture that spirit in song. It’s a song with a lot of heart, celebrating not only one remarkable puppy but also the people who recognize the importance of caring for those who care for us.

And let’s be honest. Any story that includes a puppy being sworn into a fire department deserves a song.
