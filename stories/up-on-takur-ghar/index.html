Up On Takur Ghar

Springfield's John Chapman goes back up for a fallen SEAL

John Chapman was born in Springfield and became an Air Force combat controller, the one on the ground who talks the planes in.

In March 2002, on a snowy Afghan peak called Takur Ghar, he went back up with a rescue team to find a Navy SEAL who had fallen from a helicopter. Wounded and thought lost, he kept fighting alone and drew the fire away from the rescue helicopter. Sixteen years later, his widow received his Medal of Honor.

I wrote this one for a Springfield man who believed no one gets left behind.

"Up on Takur Ghar, where the cold wind blows, / He went back up the mountain where no sane man goes."
