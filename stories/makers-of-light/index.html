Makers of Light

A song for LaunchSpace in Orange, where a class can be the prescription

In Orange, a doctor’s office can now send you to a makerspace.

LaunchSpace is a member-based makerspace and community workshop in the Orange Innovation Center at 131 West Main Street. Its SocialRx program uses “social prescribing”: instead of a pill, people are connected with classes, open shop time, a community garden and workshops, to ease loneliness and support mental health. LaunchSpace works with partners including the Franklin Regional Council of Governments, Heywood Hospital, Behavioral Health Network and the Seven Hills Foundation. Co-founder and CEO Brianna Drohen hopes area hospitals will one day prescribe a class or a membership.

Why I wrote the song

Loneliness doesn’t always look like illness, and the cure doesn’t always come in a bottle. “Makers of Light” is for the rooms where people show up empty-handed and leave with something they made.

"So bring me your silence, bring me your hands / We'll shape it together, we'll learn how to stand."
