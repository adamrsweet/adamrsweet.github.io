Amherst Gives Thanks

Thanking the Amherst crews who fought a historic town fire

On a Monday evening, Amherst's career firefighters walked into the Town Room at Town Hall to applause. With them came the call and student forces, the dispatchers, and others from public safety.

They were being recognized for their work at one of the largest fires in the town's history.

I wrote this one to add my own thanks to the people who ran in.

"Amherst gives thanks for the ones who ran in / When the fire rose high and the night turned thin"
