Connected Minds

The idea that our minds are naturally linked

I'm fascinated by the brain, by consciousness, and by what reality really is, and a lot of my songs come back to those things. Then I read about research suggesting our brains may be naturally connected, and one neuroscientist describing a conversation as a single act shared by two brains.

I wrote this one about the thought that we're never quite as separate as we feel.
