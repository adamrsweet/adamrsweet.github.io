Citrus Peaks in Winter

A pocket of summer in the gray of the year

There’s a moment every winter when the fruit bowl changes. The apples step aside, and suddenly there are clementines everywhere: in stockings, in lunch boxes, in a little pile of peels on the kitchen table.

It always strikes me as a small miracle. Just when the days are shortest here in Granby, oranges and tangerines from Florida groves and California hills hit their sweetest. Winter is citrus season.

This song is a love letter to that. The easy-peel joy of a clementine, kids with sticky hands in the schoolyard, juice by the fire. And a reminder that sometimes the best way to love something is one perfect section at a time.

The chorus says it for me:

“Citrus peaks in winter, sweet and bright,

Peel back the color, chase out the night.”

Next time you peel one, save a section for later.
