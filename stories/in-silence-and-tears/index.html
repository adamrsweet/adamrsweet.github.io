In Silence and Tears

A song for Emma MacDonald, and for the coworkers who wouldn’t let her be forgotten

This is the hardest song in the series to write about, so I'll keep it simple.

Who Emma was

Emma MacDonald worked in catering at UMass Amherst. In April 2026 she was killed at Hotel UMass. Her husband, also a UMass employee, was arrested.

Her coworkers remembered a warm smile, motherly hugs, and a way of making people feel seen. Students remembered the first person on campus who hugged them.

The vigil

Emma was a member of the University Staff Association, the union for UMass clerical and technical staff. On April 29, 2026, during the university's Founder's Day celebrations, the USA and other campus unions gathered more than 250 people at the Campus Center to grieve her. They brought flowers and candles, and resources for anyone facing domestic violence. They didn't want the campus to act like it hadn't happened.

Why I wrote it

I read about that vigil and couldn't stop thinking about it. I tried to keep the song on Emma's light, not on how she died.

What they said

"Thank you for sending this! I will share it with our board."
— Sheila Gilmour, President

If someone you know is being hurt at home, the National Domestic Violence Hotline is 1-800-799-7233, any time of day.

The bottom line

Emma's coworkers stood up and said her name. A song can't bring her back, but it can help keep saying it.
