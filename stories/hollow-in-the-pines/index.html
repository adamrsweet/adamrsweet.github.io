Hollow in the Pines

A melody carried for years, finally given form

Hollow in the Pines is a song born from a melody I wrote years ago on the violin—one that lingered quietly, waiting for the right moment.

This track carries the weight of what we bury and what still finds its way back—through dreams, memories, and sound. It’s about a place that holds your secrets, even when you’d rather they be forgotten. A place that hums and howls with truth. I layered mandolin and violin to let the strings speak where words hesitate.

For anyone who’s ever felt tethered to somewhere dark but familiar, this one's for you.
