The Fiddler’s Coming to Northampton

A song for Valley Light Opera’s first Fiddler on the Roof, and a promise to the company whose pit I sat in for years

Tevye is coming to Main Street.

What happened

Valley Light Opera, the Amherst-based company that turned 50 last year, is staging Fiddler on the Roof for the first time. It runs at the Academy of Music in Northampton on November 5 and 6 at 7:30 pm, and November 7 and 8 at 2:00 pm. A record 102 people auditioned. Joe Vincent plays Tevye. The Yiddish Book Center is helping with the dances and the history of shtetl life.

Why I wrote the song

I played viola in VLO's pit orchestra from the 1980s into the 2000s. I still miss it: the rehearsals, the people, and that moment when the overture starts and the whole room comes alive.

So when I read about this show, I wrote "The Fiddler's Coming to Northampton." The song imagines the fiddler stepping off the roof of Anatevka and landing on Main Street, bow first. The lyrics are silly on purpose.

When I sent the song to VLO, here's what came back:

"How wonderful! Thanks so much for doing this."
— Sally, Valley Light Opera

How you can help

Better still, go see the show. Buy tickets only from the Academy of Music (aomtheatre.com or 413-584-9032 ext. 105). VLO warns that other sites might be scams.

The bottom line

Community theater lives on neighbors who give their evenings to it, on stage and in the pit. L'chaim!
