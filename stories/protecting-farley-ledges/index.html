Protecting Farley Ledges

Shared ground, and the people who chose patience over shortcuts

Farley Ledges, along old Route 2, isn't just stone and routes. It's shared ground shaped by volunteers, neighbors, and a long view of access and care.

For years climbers parked where the road runs tight, waiting and playing it fair. Now seventy acres are set aside, with a trailhead planned slowly and meant to last.

Why I wrote the song

This song grew out of respect for a place and the people who chose patience over shortcuts. It's a song about stewardship that moves slowly and lasts.
