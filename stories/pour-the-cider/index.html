Pour The Cider

A song for CiderDays weekend in Franklin County

This weekend the orchards of Franklin County open their doors for CiderDays, and it seemed like the right moment to share the song I wrote for them.

For about five years, back in the late ‘80s and early ‘90s, I lived in Shelburne, above the Green River. You could hear the river roar every spring. I picked apples in those orchards, walked the roads above the river on hot days, and crossed the Bridge of Flowers more times than I can count. That part of the state still has my heart.

Pour The Cider is my thank-you to those hills. It’s about the apples rolling into the barrels, the presses still turning after the students head home, and the first mug of the season at the end of a long day. One line sums it up for me:

“From Hawley to Hadley, from the ridge to the river, the apples keep giving and the cider delivers.”

It’s one of seven songs on Harvest and Hearth, my new album of Thanksgiving songs from the Pioneer Valley. The others travel from the fields of Hadley to the Strong House in Amherst, a Chicopee fire station, the Thanksgiving morning race in East Longmeadow, and the first snow on the Seven Sisters.

If you’re heading to the orchards this weekend, raise a glass for me.
