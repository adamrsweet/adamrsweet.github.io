New Song: Waiting On The Rain

Living in western Massachusetts, it doesn’t take long before you notice when the rain stops coming.

Farmers notice it first. Gardeners notice it soon after. Streams shrink, ponds recede, and every weather forecast gets a little more attention than usual.

Even after periods of rain this spring, drought concerns have continued across parts of Massachusetts because the longer-term water deficit remains. Groundwater, streamflow, and other indicators take time to recover after extended dry periods.

That reality inspired “Waiting On The Rain.”

The song is not really about drought. It is about the people who live with it. Farmers who still plant. Gardeners who still weed. Families who watch the sky and hope the next storm will bring enough water to help the crops, gardens, rivers, and forests.

Many of us in New England have experienced years when rain seemed abundant and years when every cloud became an object of interest. Those cycles remind us how dependent we remain on natural systems despite all our modern technology.

The song reflects a simple truth that generations before us understood well. We can prepare the soil, plant the seeds, and do the work, but some things remain beyond our control.

Sometimes all we can do is wait on the rain.
