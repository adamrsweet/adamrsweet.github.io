The Cat Came Home: A Song for Springfield

Dr. Seuss's hometown welcomed the Cat back to the big screen

On Friday, November 6, 2026, families in Springfield gathered at the Regal Cinemas at MGM Springfield for a premiere screening of the new animated The Cat in the Hat. Mayor Domenic Sarno joined staff from the Springfield Museums to celebrate the Cat coming home to the city where Theodor Geisel grew up.

That’s the picture behind my new song, The Cat Came Home: little ones in striped hats with stuffed cats held tight, Thing One and Thing Two, and a grandpa in the back row remembering the book from bedtime more than sixty years ago.

Oh, the Cat came home to Springfield,

Red hat and all,

Where Dr. Seuss grew up and wrote

The books we all recall.

The book came out in 1957, so it turns seventy next year. From Mulberry Street to the movie screen, the Cat still feels brand new to every kid who meets him.

I kept this one short and simple, something for the kitchen table or the car ride home.
