New Song: Rusty Old Tin Can

Some songs come from big moments in life. Others come from ordinary days and ordinary people.

“Rusty Old Tin Can” is about finding happiness even when life is far from perfect. The character in the song has a hole in his pocket, a tear in his shoe, and not much money to spare. Still, he wakes up with a smile, keeps moving forward, and finds joy in the simple things.

I think a lot of us know what that feels like. Life leaves its dents and scratches on everyone. Plans don’t always work out. Money comes and goes. We all carry a few bumps from the road behind us. Yet there is something admirable about people who keep a good attitude and make the best of what they have.

The title came from the idea that a rusty old tin can may not look like much, but it can still make a joyful sound. In a way, that simple image became the heart of the song.

At its core, “Rusty Old Tin Can” is a song about being content with who you are, appreciating what you have, and finding a reason to sing no matter what comes your way.
