New Song: Protecting Hampshire's Landscape

Kestrel Land Trust, along with The Trustees of Reservations, has stepped forward with a strong plan to protect the expansive rural landscape around Hampshire College.

These beautiful lands include meadows, forests, and active farmland that have long been part of Hampshire’s identity. The proposal aims to permanently conserve these open spaces, support local agriculture, protect wildlife habitats, and keep the area available for education and community use.

This effort comes at an important time as the college considers its future. Instead of seeing the land developed or sold off completely, this vision focuses on thoughtful conservation that balances nature and community needs.

I wrote “Protecting Hampshire Landscape” to celebrate this positive step and to honor the people working hard to preserve this special place for years to come.

The song is a reminder that some places are worth protecting so future generations can still walk the same trails, see the same fields, and feel connected to the land.

Stream “Protecting Hampshire Landscape” and feel the hope for these beautiful acres.
