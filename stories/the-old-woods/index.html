The Old Woods

Twelve hundred acres of Pioneer Valley forest protected for good

More than 1,200 acres of forest across six Pioneer Valley towns are being permanently conserved, protecting woods, streams, wetlands, wildlife habitat, and drinking water. In a time when land is measured by what can be built on it, these woods get to stay woods.

I wrote this one thinking about the animals crossing that land, who'll never know somebody signed papers to protect it. Maybe that's the best kind of legacy.

"Just leave a little wild land / For the ones who come this way."
