The Gray House on Sheldon Street

Springfield's Gray House and its Thanksgiving promise to the North End

In Springfield's North End, five sisters bought a fire-scarred, empty gray house on Sheldon Street back in '82. For more than forty years it has made sure neighbors facing hard times don't face them alone, with a market that feeds thousands each month.

Every November, the Gray House gathers turkeys, sides, and pie so local families can sit down to a full Thanksgiving at home. Last year that was 717 households.

I wrote this one for a simple promise kept: nobody should wonder where the next meal is coming from when the table is meant to be full.

"Oh the Gray House, the Gray House, on Sheldon Street / Where hope gets a second helping and the welcome’s always free"
