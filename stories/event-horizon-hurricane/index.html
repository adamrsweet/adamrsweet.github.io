New Song: Event Horizon Hurricane

Physics has long been one of my favorite sources of musical inspiration. The universe routinely produces phenomena that are far stranger and more beautiful than anything we could invent.

Recently I read about the quasar J2318, where astronomers observed an ultraviolet wind moving at nearly 30 percent of the speed of light. The outflow originates near a supermassive black hole more than a billion times the mass of our Sun. Even by astronomical standards, the scale is difficult to comprehend.

That discovery became the starting point for “Event Horizon Hurricane.”

In “Event Horizon Hurricane,” the landscape is a black hole at the center of a distant quasar, hurling matter across the universe at unimaginable speeds.
