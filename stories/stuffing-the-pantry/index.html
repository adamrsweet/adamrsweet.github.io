Stuffing the Pantry

East Longmeadow's Thanksgiving morning 5K for the Open Pantry

On Thanksgiving morning in East Longmeadow, runners and walkers line up on Chestnut Street for the 15th annual Stuffing the Pantry 5K.

Every step supports the Open Pantry and neighbors facing food insecurity.

I wrote this one for the shelves that stay full and the real meaning of the holiday.

"We stuff the pantry so the shelves won’t run bare / For the hungry and the homeless and the ones who need care"
