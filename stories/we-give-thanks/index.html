We Give Thanks

Amherst's old Strong House on Amity Hill, still standing

The Strong House has stood on Amity Hill in Amherst for a very long time. Mabel later opened its rooms and kept the Dickinson lines, and Sarah left the four-poster in place so the house would remember.

This autumn the floors came up again to show the old joists and stone, and the north wall had been sinking. The work goes on, and the museum will open in the spring.

I wrote this one to give thanks for the bones of an old house that the years couldn't break.

"We give thanks for the bones that the years couldn’t break / For the hands that still tend what the first builders made"
