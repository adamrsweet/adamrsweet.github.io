New Song: The River Keepers

Song ideas often come from unexpected places. This one came from reading about freshwater mussels.

Most people never see them. They spend their lives buried in riverbeds, slowly filtering water and helping keep aquatic ecosystems healthy. Yet a single mussel can filter gallons of water each day, and large mussel beds can clean rivers on a scale that is almost hard to imagine.

What surprised me most was learning how many species are in trouble. North America is home to an extraordinary diversity of freshwater mussels, but many are endangered or threatened by pollution, habitat loss, and changes to river systems.

The good news is that conservation efforts are working. Scientists and restoration teams are breeding mussels and returning them to rivers where they once thrived. In many cases, healthier mussel populations lead to cleaner water and stronger ecosystems.

“The Keepers of the River” is a song inspired by that story. The spotlight falls on a humble creature that spends its life quietly making the world a little better.

The song is also a reminder that conservation is not only about protecting the large and charismatic animals that make headlines. Sometimes the most important work is being done by creatures hidden beneath the water, carrying out their task day after day, year after year.

The next time you stand beside a clear river, there is a good chance that freshwater mussels are helping make it that way
