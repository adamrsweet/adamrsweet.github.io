Three New Songs, Straight From the Amherst Indy

Art Keene wrote about the songs the Indy's reporting inspired

The Amherst Indy ran a piece yesterday by Art Keene about three new songs I wrote from stories I read in its pages. I’m grateful to the Indy and to the reporters whose work got me writing.

• The Hard Way, from Jeff Lee’s piece on Great Barrington’s Ramsdell Library getting a crash course in what Amherst learned the hard way with the Jones Library expansion.

• Stone Soup Café (Everybody’s Seat), from Chloe Spurr and Rowan Faris’s piece on Stone Soup Café’s red door on Hope Street in Greenfield.

• Coronation Café (Room at the Table), from the Indy’s report on Coronation Café extending its table, ahead of Thursday’s ribbon cutting.

As I told the Indy, writing a song is a lot like journalism. You have to talk to the people involved.

A reader, Hetty Startup, also left a lovely comment noting that my East Amherst song was mentioned on the Mass Historical Preservation list serve.
