Little Food Bank

A Holyoke girl's answer when SNAP benefits stopped

On November 1, thousands of people in Massachusetts lost their SNAP benefits. One girl in Holyoke decided to do something about it in her own community. She painted an old box bright, set it by a maple tree, and filled it with cans and bread for anyone who needed them.

I wrote this one for her, and for every grown-up who learned something from a kid that week.

"Little food bank by the road, / Take what you need, lighten your load."
