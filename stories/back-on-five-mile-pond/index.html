Back on Five Mile Pond: A Song for Springfield's Open Water

The boat launch on Boston Road is open again

Five Mile Pond sits in Springfield’s Pine Point neighborhood, with Boston Road traffic on one side and trees and open water on the other. Since June 1, the boat launch and fishing area at 980 Boston Road had been closed for renovations. This week the gates opened again.

The city added accessible fishing decks, a repaved parking lot, new trees, benches and bike racks, so more people can get to the water, young and old. Tom Ashe, Springfield’s parks director, says the city is working to keep the water consistently clean. Next comes the beach side, with a $1 million federal Land and Water Conservation Fund grant matched by the city.

That reopening is where my new song, Back on Five Mile Pond, begins.

Paddle soft on Five Mile Pond, where the open spaces call,

Keep the water clean and clear for the heron, the bass, and all.

In the middle of Springfield town there’s a place to breathe and be,

With a paddle in my hands and the sky above the trees.

It’s a song for anyone who loves clean water and open space that doesn’t need much improving to be worth keeping.
