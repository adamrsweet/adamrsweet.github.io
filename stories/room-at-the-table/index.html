Room at the Table

A song for Coronation Café’s ribbon cutting in downtown Amherst, Thursday, Nov. 5

Coronation Café just made its table bigger, and everybody's invited.

Coronation Café opened in 2022 on North Pleasant Street in Amherst, in the old Bart's Ice Cream spot. Owner Joe Kurtz built it around good food and real conversation. This year they doubled the space, added a private room for gatherings, and opened an ice cream window serving Maple Valley Creamery. They're celebrating with a ribbon cutting on Thursday, Nov. 5, from 11 a.m. to noon at 103 N. Pleasant St.

Why I wrote the song

I spent years in Amherst. I went to Hampshire, lived on Baker Street, and taught at Fretted Instruments and Red Barn Music. North Pleasant Street has been part of my life for a long time, and I love seeing a place there grow by making room for more people. "Coronation Café (Room at the Table)" is a sing-along with a chorus made for a crowd on a sidewalk.

"There's room at the table, pull up a chair / There's room at the table, we'll meet you there."
