Road Remembers

An ancient statue from Sardis, buried face-down in a Roman road

At Sardis, in what is now Turkey, archaeologists found a nearly seven-foot statue once raised to honor a powerful man. It was later broken, turned face-down, and laid into a Roman road, where it vanished under centuries of footsteps and wheels.

I wrote this one for the irony of that journey: power becomes stone, stone becomes pavement, and the stone outlasts the empire.

"Oh, the road remembers what the kings forget / Every crown comes down to earth eventually yet"
