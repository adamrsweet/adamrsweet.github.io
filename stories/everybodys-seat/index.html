Everybody’s Seat

A sing-along for Stone Soup Café in Greenfield, where nobody is a giver or a taker

Behind a red door on Hope Street in Greenfield, there's a table that never turns anyone away.

What happened

Stone Soup Café started in 2010 as a weekly community meal at All Souls Church, with a little help from Bernie Glassman and Jeff Bridges. The idea was simple: pay what you can, take what you need, and sit down together. Fifteen years later it has grown into Saturday dinners, a soup CSA, catering, baked goods at the farmers' market and a culinary training program, with Rachel's Table and Just Roots bringing the harvest in. They still dream of buying a building of their own.

Why I wrote the song

I loved the way they talk about it. No one is a "giver" or a "taker." Everyone just has a seat. "Stone Soup Café (Everybody's Seat)" is a sing-along for a room full of people with spoons in their hands.

"Give what you can and take what you need / Stone Soup Café's got room enough to spare."

How you can help

Learn the chorus and bring it to a soup night. Better yet, volunteer a shift or drop something in the pot.
