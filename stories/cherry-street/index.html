Cherry Street

For the veterans of the Soldiers' Home in Holyoke

In the spring of 2020, seventy-six veterans died at the Soldiers' Home in Holyoke while their families waited outside, unable to say goodbye.

The families wouldn't let it rest. They kept asking why, through the hearings and the headlines and the long years after.

A new home on the hill

Six years later, on that same hillside off Cherry Street, a new veterans home has opened. It began welcoming residents on November 10, 2026, the day before Veterans Day.

Why I wrote the song

This song is for the ones we lost, for the families who kept asking why, and for the promise that no one who served will be left waiting again.
