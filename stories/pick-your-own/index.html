New Song: Pick Your Own

One of my favorite signs of summer in western Massachusetts is the appearance of hand-painted signs announcing strawberry season.

For a few short weeks each June, local farms invite people into the fields to gather their own berries. Families arrive with baskets, children race down the rows searching for the biggest strawberries, and everyone leaves with something fresh, local, and delicious.

The experience is about much more than fruit.

Pick-your-own farms create a connection between people and the land. They remind us where our food comes from and help support the farmers who work throughout the year to bring it to our tables. They also create opportunities for families and neighbors to spend time together outdoors and participate in a tradition that has been part of New England life for generations.

After reading about this year’s strawberry season, I was inspired to write “Pick Your Own.” The song celebrates the sights, sounds, and simple pleasures of a day spent in the fields. It is also a tribute to the local farms that help define the character of our communities.

Many of my songs are inspired by conservation, nature, and community. This story sits right at the intersection of all three. A healthy local farm is more than a business. It is part of the landscape, part of the culture, and part of the shared experience of living in this region.

Strawberry season may only last a few weeks, but the memories often last much longer.
