Memorial Hall

Steve Connor's quiet work housing veterans from Northampton's Memorial Hall

Not every veteran's hero wears a medal. For twenty years Steve Connor worked out of the basement of Memorial Hall in Northampton, going to Housing Court on Monday mornings and pushing paperwork until veterans had a door and a key.

One of them was Lidon, a Vietnam veteran who had spent years in a shelter until Steve found him a home in Florence. Steve retired this spring, and now Lidon helps lead the board that serves other veterans.

I wrote this one about how kindness travels on, from one man to the next.

"It ain't medals, it ain't marching, it's a door, a bed, a call, / It's the quiet work of keeping faith down at Memorial Hall."
