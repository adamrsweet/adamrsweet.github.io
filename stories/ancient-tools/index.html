Ancient Tools

Southeast Asia's first sailors, forty thousand years ago

Stone tools from sites in Southeast Asia show that people there were building boats and fishing the open sea some 40,000 years ago.

That puts them ahead of Europe and Africa, and it changes how we tell the story of who led the way.

I wrote this one for the first sailors who never made it into the history books.

"Southeast waters knew the way, / Light on the bows at the break of day."
