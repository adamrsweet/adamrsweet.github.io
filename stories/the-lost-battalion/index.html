The Lost Battalion

Pittsfield's Charles Whittlesey and five days cut off in the Argonne

Charles Whittlesey grew up in Pittsfield, a quiet young man in wire-rimmed glasses who would rather read than fight.

In October 1918 he led 554 men into a ravine in the Argonne Forest, where they were cut off for five days with no food or water. He wouldn't surrender, and 194 of them walked out. He was given the Medal of Honor on Boston Common on Christmas Eve, but the war never really left him.

I wrote this one for every quiet veteran who doesn't talk about it.

"So when you see a quiet soldier who won't talk about the war, / Sit beside him for a while, that's what Veterans Day is for."
