New Song: Alex's Lemonade

Sometimes the most inspiring stories come from ordinary people doing extraordinary things.

I recently read about Jace, a childhood cancer survivor from Belchertown, who is using a lemonade stand to raise money for childhood cancer research and to support families currently facing the challenges he once faced himself.

What struck me was the spirit behind the effort. This is not just a fundraiser. It is a celebration of resilience, gratitude, and community. Having come through a difficult journey, Jace wants to help other children and families still traveling that road.

The story also connects to the remarkable legacy of Alex Scott, whose lemonade stands grew into a foundation that continues to fund childhood cancer research and support families across the country. Her idea was simple, but its impact has been enormous.

“Alex’s Lemonade” grew from these stories of generosity and hope. The song focuses on the idea that small acts can have a lasting effect. A cup of lemonade may seem like a simple thing, but when it is offered with compassion and purpose, it becomes something much bigger.

Many of the songs I write are inspired by community stories, and this one is a perfect example. It reminds us that strength is not only measured by what we overcome, but also by how we choose to help others afterward.

In a world that can often feel divided, a child standing behind a lemonade stand to help other children offers a powerful reminder of what community is all about.
