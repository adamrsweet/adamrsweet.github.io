Message by Bottle

WWI soldiers' bottled messages found a century later

Two Australian soldiers, a few days into their voyage to the battlefields of France in World War I, wrote messages and sealed them in a bottle.

More than a century later, those messages were found on Australia's coast.

I wrote this one for two young men whose words finally made it home.

"Message by bottle, carried by tide, / Time stood still on that endless ride,"
